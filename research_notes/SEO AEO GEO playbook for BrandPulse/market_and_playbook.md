# AI Visibility (AEO/GEO) Agency Market and Service Playbook: India SMBs and B2B SaaS (as of Oct 2026)

Research date: 2026-10-23. Unless a source is primary (company filing, Google/Pew/OpenAI), assume figures are **vendor- or agency-authored and self-interested**. Pricing "ranges" for named agencies are mostly third-party estimates, not published price lists.

## 1. Who the AEO/GEO agencies are, what they offer and charge, and their guarantee stance

### Takeaway
Global GEO is sold mainly as a 3–6+ month retainer. Typical prices are about $3k–$20k/mo, with a $1.5k–$5k one-off audit as the entry product. Indian agencies cluster at ₹20k–₹3.5L/mo. Credible players **do not guarantee AI placements or rankings**. They commit to scope and volume (articles, pitches, prompts tracked) instead of outcomes.

### Cited Findings
**Global price ranges (all agency/vendor blogs, 2026)**
- A Red-engage compilation of 20 agencies (Jun 15, 2026; Red-engage is itself one of the 20 listed agencies, so it is self-interested) gives these figures, with only Red-engage's own prices confirmed: Red-engage $4,500 / $7,000 / $14,000 per month (5 / 8 / 12 articles per month, Reddit placements, PR on the top tier) and a $2,500 standalone audit. Estimates for the others: Sure Oak $3k–$20k; First Page Sage $8k–$20k; SimpleTiger ~$5k–$12k (retainer plus project); TripleDart $8k+ ("pipeline attribution tied to AI citations"); Omniscient Digital, NoGood, Single Grain, Foundation, Siege Media and Animalz each $10k+; Embarque $3k–$8k; iPullRank and Terakeet enterprise at six figures a year. "Most GEO retainers require a minimum commitment, typically three months." None of the 20 states a guarantee. — [Red-engage, GEO Agency Pricing 2026](https://red-engage.com/blog/geo-agency-pricing)
- Market-wide ranges: B2B GEO retainers $5k–$12k/mo, entry programs $3k–$5k, full-service $10k–$20k; one-off audits $1.5k–$5k; annual contracts 10–20% cheaper than month-to-month — [VisibilityStack](https://www.visibilitystack.ai/signals/article/geo-agency-pricing-b2b-saas); [Mentionable](https://mentionable.ai/en/blog/geo-agency-cost-pricing); [Derivatex](https://derivatex.agency/blog/geo-agency-pricing-month-to-month-vs-annual-retainer/)

- Getspotted packaging model (Jul 12, 2026; vendor), three tiers:
  - (1) Audit, $1.5k–$4k one-off: citation map per keyword and country, share-of-voice baseline vs the top 3 competitors, "hotspot" sources, ranked gap list, 5–10 prioritized actions.
  - (2) Monitoring retainer, $1.5k/$3k/$5k per month for 50/150/400 keywords.
  - (3) Source-outreach retainer, $4k–$12k/mo: 10–40 target sources per month and 15–30 pitches.
  - The model commits to "outreach volume, not guaranteed placements". It describes audit → monitoring → outreach as the usual upsell path. — [Getspotted](https://getspotted.ai/blog/geo-agency-retainer-pricing-playbook)

**India price ranges (agency-authored)**

Typical combined SEO+GEO retainers run ₹50k–₹1.5L/mo; startups and local businesses about ₹25k–₹40k/mo; one-off AI visibility audits from about ₹25k–₹40k; prompt-tracking tools alone ₹1,999–₹25k/mo. — [Adexorb](https://adexorb.com/best-geo-agency-in-india/); [DigiMark](https://www.digimarkagency.com/blog/7-best-ai-seo-agencies-in-india-for-2026-pricing-geo-and-aeo-compared/); [Mediaverse](https://www.themediaverse.in/digital-marketing/ai-seo); [DigiStreet](https://digistreetmedia.com/ai-seo-agency-india/); [TheBillionGame](https://www.thebilliongame.com/blog/aeo-pricing-india); [Digiflute](https://www.digiflute.com/blogs/seo-geo-services-india-2026-market-size-pricing-roi/)

### Inferences
- There is a clear gap between ₹20k–₹50k/mo "SEO + GEO" bundles (probably repackaged SEO) and ₹1L+/mo specialist retainers. A transparent, scoped done-for-you **sprint** at about ₹50k–₹1.5L fits a gap most Indian competitors leave open.
- "Free audit → paid sprint → optional retainer" matches the market's dominant path (audit → monitoring → outreach). Giving the audit away undercuts competitors who charge ₹25k–₹8L for one.
- Refusing guarantees is industry-normal for credible agencies, so it costs BrandPulse no credibility.

### Gaps
- I did not fetch first-party service pages for TripleDart, SimpleTiger, Omniscient, iPullRank or First Page Sage. Their deliverables and guarantee language come from secondary listings only.
- No Clutch or GoodFirms verified price data was retrieved.

Google AI Overviews (English and Hindi since Aug 2024) and AI Mode (Hindi plus 7 Indian languages since Oct 2025) put AI answers in front of mainstream searchers. ## 3. Case studies of AI visibility improvements with real numbers

### Takeaway
Public case studies are few, mostly vendor-published and anonymized. The most-cited example is Ramp: 3.2% to 22.2% AI visibility in one month after rebuilding comparison pages with structured data, as documented by Profound. Evidence is weak overall, and AI answers are volatile, so any before/after must use repeated sampling over a fixed prompt set.

### Cited Findings
- **Ramp** (Profound case study, via secondary summary): rebuilt its corporate-card comparison pages with structured competitor data and verifiable statistics. AI visibility went from 3.2% to 22.2% in about a month. — [The DataStory (Substack)](https://thedatastory.substack.com/p/case-study-1-its-profound). I did not verify the primary Profound page.
- **Rankio anonymized cases** (unverified marketing): B2B SaaS share of voice 3% to 19% in 6 weeks; fintech 7% to 22% in 8 weeks; healthcare 2% to 31% in 10 weeks. No named clients or method. — [Rankio](https://www.rankio.studio/learn/case-studies)
- **upGrowth timeline claims** (agency): AI visibility lags 30–60 days after publishing; "first citation wins typically appear between day 45 and day 90"; 3–5x citation-share growth on primary prompts takes 6–9 months. — [upGrowth](https://upgrowth.in/geo-aeo-pricing-benchmark-india-2026/)
- **Volatility:** ChatGPT's Reddit citation share reportedly fell from about 60% to about 10% in mid-Sep 2025. — [Discovered Labs / search summary](https://discoveredlabs.com/blog/profound-ai-visibility-tool-review)
- **SparkToro and Gumshoe study** (Jan 2026): 600 volunteers ran 12 prompts 2,961 times on ChatGPT, Claude and Google AI (Nov–Dec 2025).
  - Repeatability: under 1 in 100 chance that two runs return the same brand list; about 1 in 1,000 for the same order.
  - Appearance rate was more stable. Top brands appeared in 55–77% of responses.
  - The authors recommend tracking the percentage of responses that mention a brand, not its rank.
  - Not peer reviewed. — [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/ai-recommendations-change-with-nearly-every-query-sparktoro/566242)
- **Ahrefs** (75k brands, AI Overviews): the strongest correlates of AI Overview brand mentions are off-site. Branded web mentions 0.664, branded anchors 0.527, branded search volume 0.392 (Spearman). Ahrefs calls all factors moderate to very weak correlations. — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overview-brand-correlation/)

### Inferences
- BrandPulse's own audits (presence rate across N prompts) match SparkToro's recommended metric. Ranks within answers should be de-emphasized.
- For credible before/after evidence, run each prompt several times, keep the prompt set fixed, and report confidence ranges. Single-run deltas can be noise.
- An honest promise is "first movement in 6–12 weeks, durable gains in 3–6+ months", not "results in 2 weeks".

### Gaps
- No independently audited case study found. No India-specific published case study with numbers.

## 4. The done-for-you playbook: deliverables, 30/60/90-day sequencing and reporting

### Takeaway
Agencies converge on this order: baseline audit and prompt set → technical and schema fixes plus entity cleanup → on-site answer content (comparison, service, FAQ pages) → off-site authority (directories, reviews, digital PR and listicle inclusion, Reddit/YouTube) → monthly re-measurement. Off-site brand mentions are the strongest correlate of AI visibility, so a program limited to on-site work underdelivers.

### Cited Findings
- **Deliverables by tier.** India: ₹1L/mo covers tracking on 3 platforms, 4 GEO articles, basic schema and a quarterly benchmark; ₹2L/mo adds technical SEO, internal linking and 2–3 PR placements. Global: Red-engage tiers bundle articles per month, Reddit placements, quarterly or monthly content refreshes and PR. — [upGrowth](https://upgrowth.in/geo-aeo-pricing-benchmark-india-2026/); [Red-engage](https://red-engage.com/blog/geo-agency-pricing)
- **Sequencing.** The schema and technical audit comes within the first 30 days; first citation wins come around day 45–90. — [upGrowth](https://upgrowth.in/geo-aeo-pricing-benchmark-india-2026/)
- **Reporting cadence and metrics.** Monthly, bi-weekly or weekly by tier. Metrics: citations per engine, share of voice vs named competitors, hotspot sources, gap list, competitor movement. — [Getspotted](https://getspotted.ai/blog/geo-agency-retainer-pricing-playbook)
- **Off-site weight.** Branded web mentions are the top AI Overview correlate (0.664). — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overview-brand-correlation/)
- **Content that worked.** Structured comparison pages with verifiable statistics (the Ramp case). — [The DataStory](https://thedatastory.substack.com/p/case-study-1-its-profound)
- **Indian directories.** Justdial has 56.1M active listings and 192.9M quarterly visitors; IndiaMART has 8.8M storefronts. Both are large surfaces that AI and Google index. — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/markets/stocks/story/just-dial-shares-jump-nearly-15-on-q1-fy27-earnings-details-here-542458-2026-07-13); [Kalkine](https://www.kalkine.co.in/article/announcements/indiamart-nseindiamart-q1-fy27-results-show-10-revenue-growth-and-strong-deferred-revenue-build)

### Inferences (a synthesized 90-day plan for BrandPulse; not from a single source)
- **Days 0–30:**
  - Fix the measurement baseline: freeze the prompt set and run it multiple times per engine.
  - Technical and entity work: crawlability for AI bots, Organization/LocalBusiness/FAQ schema, a consistent business name, address and phone everywhere.
  - Claim and complete Google Business Profile, Justdial/IndiaMART/Practo/Sulekha (local) or G2/Capterra/Clutch/GoodFirms (SaaS and agencies).
  - Start a legitimate review-request flow.
- **Days 31–60:**
  - Publish answer-first service pages, FAQ hubs, "X vs Y" and "alternatives" comparison pages, and city/area pages backed by real data.
  - Pitch inclusion in third-party "best X in city" lists and roundups, earned only and never paid undisclosed.
  - Seed genuine Reddit/Quora/YouTube answers.
- **Days 61–90:** Digital PR or original data (for example a small local survey), refresh content based on missed prompts, and re-run the audit. Report the presence-rate change with variance and wins per prompt.
- **Monthly report:** presence rate and share of voice vs named competitors, by engine; newly won and lost prompts with quotes; cited-domain gaps closed; work shipped. Add business proxies such as GBP calls, branded search and enquiries. Positioning: "we commit to work shipped and measure honestly".

### Gaps
- No first-party 30/60/90 template from a named top agency was retrieved. The plan above is synthesized.
- No data on the effectiveness of llms.txt or AI-specific schema. Treat both as low-confidence and low-cost hygiene.

## 5. Ethics, legal risk and credible wording of audit findings

### Takeaway
Google extended its spam policies to manipulation of AI Overviews and AI Mode in May 2026. Self-promotional "we're #1" listicles appear to be losing value and carrying risk. In India, misleading ads, false guarantees and fake reviews fall under the Consumer Protection Act 2019, the CCPA 2022 misleading-ads guidelines (penalty up to ₹10L, ₹50L for repeat offences) and the BIS IS 19000:2022 review standard (voluntary). BrandPulse should not guarantee AI placements, must not fake reviews or buy undisclosed listicle spots, and should label audit scores as specific to the prompt set, engine and date.

### Cited Findings
- **Google, May 2026.** Several sources report that Google's spam policies were expanded effective May 15, 2026 to explicitly cover attempts to manipulate generative AI responses (AI Overviews and AI Mode). Google has not named self-promotional listicles specifically. Mass self-ranking lists risk the "scaled content abuse" policy. These are secondary blogs; I did not fetch Google's primary page. — [Cicero Studio](https://cicero.studio/en/blog/google-june-2026-spam-update-ai-citation-manipulation-2026/); [Aivy](https://aivy.com.au/news/google-ai-overviews-spam/); [Promptwatch glossary](https://promptwatch.com/glossary/self-promotional-listicles)
- **Lily Ray's findings** (reported second-hand): 30–50% organic drops for SaaS/B2B sites self-ranking in listicles after the Dec 2025 core update. A Sep 2026 analysis of 100 queries found AI Overviews cited 38% fewer self-promotional listicles than in June and excluded the self-ranked brand 83% of the time. Small sample, unverified, other factors possible. — [Bushletter](https://www.bushletter.com/google-ai-overviews-cite-38-fewer-vendor-listicles-data-shows/); [Lily Ray Substack](https://lilyraynyc.substack.com/p/prediction-the-next-massive-google); [Coalition Technologies](https://coalitiontechnologies.com/blog/self-promotion-listicles-for-ai-seo)
- **CCPA guidelines.** Notified Jun 9, 2022; they apply to manufacturers, service providers, advertisers and advertising agencies. The Consumer Protection Act 2019 defines a misleading ad to include a false guarantee. Penalty up to ₹10 lakh, and up to ₹50 lakh for repeat violations. Pre-2022, ASCI codes were self-regulatory and not enforceable. — [SCC Online](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2022/07/07/changing-how-india-advertises-government-introduces-new-guidelines/); [IndiaLaw](https://www.indialaw.in/blog/civil/ccpa-misleading-ads-endorsements/); [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/india/advertising-marketing-branding/1214408/new-guidelines-to-curb-misleading-advertisements-in-india)
- **Fake reviews.** BIS IS 19000:2022 covers any organisation publishing consumer reviews, including sellers collecting their own reviews, and it is voluntary as of a Mar 2025 law-firm note. The ₹10L–₹50L fake-review penalty widely quoted was a 2022 proposal I could not confirm was enacted. Fake reviews remain actionable as an unfair trade practice. — [JSA Law](https://www.jsalaw.com/newsletters-and-updates/bis-standard-on-online-consumer-reviews/); [CAG blog](https://www.cag.org.in/blogs/regulating-trust-indias-legal-response-fake-online-reviews); [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/news/govt-announces-new-norms-to-curb-fake-online-reviews-of-products-services-news-239037)
- **No guarantees as norm.** upGrowth calls guaranteed top-3 AI citations "meaningless" without scope. Getspotted commits to "outreach volume, not guaranteed placements". — [upGrowth](https://upgrowth.in/geo-aeo-pricing-benchmark-india-2026/); [Getspotted](https://getspotted.ai/blog/geo-agency-retainer-pricing-playbook)
- **Measurement honesty.** AI lists rarely repeat (under 1/100). Visibility percentage is the more stable metric. — [SEJ on SparkToro](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/ai-recommendations-change-with-nearly-every-query-sparktoro/566242)

### Inferences (wording guidance for BrandPulse audits)
- Label every score in this form: "Measured on [N] buyer questions we wrote for [category, city], asked to [engine(s)] on [date]. AI answers vary run to run, and a re-run may differ by several points."
- Present the score as a sample of AI answers, not "your AI ranking".
- Show the real quote and the prompt as evidence. Never paraphrase a competitor mention into something stronger.
- Do not say "AI is ignoring you" when the engine simply returned generic advice. Report "no businesses named" separately from "competitors named, you were not".
- Promise to ship work and to measure; never promise placements, rankings or lead numbers. Avoid "guaranteed", "#1 on ChatGPT" or "get recommended in 30 days" in ads, as these risk the CCPA false-guarantee provision.
- Health clinics (YMYL): avoid outcome claims, and keep in mind sector-specific medical advertising rules (not researched here).
- Review generation: ask all customers (no gating or incentives tied to positive reviews) and never write reviews.
- Listicle placement: earned or clearly disclosed; never pay for undisclosed rankings.

### Gaps
- I did not fetch Google's primary spam-policy text for the May 2026 change.
- I did not retrieve ASCI's specific codes on digital or AI-marketing claims, or NMC/medical advertising rules for clinics.

## 6. Pricing and packaging relevant to Indian SMB budgets

### Takeaway

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- **Local SMBs:** a fixed-scope 6–8 week "fix sprint" is the likely fit. It would cover GBP, directories, schema, 5–10 answer pages, a review flow and a re-audit, at about ₹30k–₹75k one-off, with an optional light monthly upkeep. This is my inference, not a sourced price. It is consistent with the memory note that BrandPulse scopes on calls with no public prices.
- **SaaS / B2B:** a 90-day sprint at about ₹1.5L–₹4L, then a retainer, aligns with upGrowth's ₹3L–₹4L "minimum viable sprint" and with global entry tiers.
- The free audit is a real differentiator, since competitors charge ₹25k–₹8L for one. Its credibility depends on transparent methodology (section 5).
- Avoid performance-only pricing early. Measurement is noisy (SparkToro), which invites disputes.
