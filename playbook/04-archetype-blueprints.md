# 04. Archetype Blueprints: Exactly Which Pages to Build

After the diagnosis in [02](02-business-diagnosis.md), pick the blueprint for the primary archetype, then add pages from the secondary archetype. Each blueprint gives:

- **Launch set**: pages to build in the first 30 days (the minimum credible site)
- **Expansion set**: pages for days 31 to 90
- **Ongoing**: monthly cadence after that
- **Off-site priorities** for this archetype
- **Typical page count** at day 90

Template codes (T1 to T30) refer to [03-page-templates.md](03-page-templates.md).

**Counting rule:** a site with 5 pages cannot win a query universe of 100 questions. But every page must deserve to exist. If you cannot write something specific and useful for a page, do not build it yet.

## A. Local service, single location

*Dentist, salon, CA firm, physiotherapist, gym, lawyer, interior designer, plumber.*

### Launch set (days 1 to 30): about 20 to 25 pages
| Page | Template | Count |
|---|---|---|
| Homepage | T1 | 1 |
| About | T2 | 1 |
| Person pages (owner + each practitioner) | T4 | 2 to 5 |
| Services hub | T7 | 1 |
| Service detail pages (every service that brings revenue) | T8 | 8 to 15 |
| Pricing (or price list with ranges) | T9 | 1 |
| Contact / Book | T5 | 1 |
| Reviews hub | T18 | 1 |
| Policies (cancellation, privacy, terms) | T6 | 2 to 3 |

### Expansion (days 31 to 90): +20 to 30 pages
| Page | Template | Count |
|---|---|---|
| Cost guides for top 5 services | T23 | 5 |
| Option comparisons ("veneers vs bonding", "aligners vs braces") | T12 | 3 to 5 |
| Case studies / patient stories (with consent) | T17 | 3 to 5 |
| Area pages for neighborhoods genuinely served, with real local substance | T10 | 0 to 5 |
| Pillar guide for the main category | T19 | 1 |
| Question articles from the fan-out list | T21 | 6 to 10 |
| Brand facts page | T3 | 1 |

### Ongoing
- 2 to 4 articles/month answering real patient/client questions.
- Refresh cost guides every 6 months. Add case studies as they happen.
- Weekly Google Business Profile posts, photo uploads, Q&A.

### Off-site priorities (often more important than the site for local)
1. **Google Business Profile**: correct primary category, all secondary categories, every service listed with descriptions and prices, hours, photos (30+), attributes, booking link, weekly posts. Link GBP to the location/contact page.
2. **Reviews engine**: ask every happy customer; target a steady flow (for example 8 to 15/month) rather than bursts. Reply to every review. Reviews that mention the service and area by name help.
3. **Local and vertical directories with consistent NAP**: India: Justdial, Sulekha, IndiaMART (B2B), Practo/Lybrate (health), Zomato (food), UrbanClap/Urban Company (home services). Global: Yelp, Apple Business Connect, Bing Places, Facebook, industry directories.
4. **Local media and community**: local news features, neighborhood blogs, city subreddits and Facebook groups (helpful participation, never spam), local events and sponsorships.
5. **Apple Business Connect and Bing Places** (feeds Siri, Apple Maps, Copilot).

### Day-90 page count: 40 to 55

## B. Multi-location local

*Dental chain, coaching institute with branches, clinic network, restaurant group, franchise.*

Everything in A, plus:

| Page | Template | Count |
|---|---|---|
| Locations index (by city, with map) | T7-style hub | 1 |
| City pages (one per city with 2+ branches) | T10 | per city |
| Branch pages (one per physical location, unique content) | T10 | per branch |
| Service x City pages for top services in top cities | T11 | 3 to 5 services x top cities |
| Doctor/staff pages linked to their branch | T4 | per practitioner |

**Rules for scale:**
- One GBP per branch, each linking to its own branch page.
- Each branch page must have unique facts: address, hours, team, photos, branch-specific reviews, local directions, local services. Use a template for structure, never for content. **[OFFICIAL]** Mass-produced pages with swapped city names are doorway pages.
- Build a central data source (branch name, address, hours, services, staff) that feeds pages, schema, GBP, and directory listings, so NAP stays consistent everywhere.
- Rollout: launch top-revenue branches first, measure, then expand.

### Day-90 page count: 60 to 300+ depending on branches

### Expansion (days 31 to 90): +30 to 60 pages
| Page | Template | Count |
|---|---|---|
| Industry pages | T15 | 3 to 5 |
| More comparisons (competitor vs competitor too: "HubSpot vs Salesforce" from a neutral angle with you as a third option) | T12 | 5 to 10 |
| Case studies | T17 | 3 to 5 |
| Pillar guides for the core job-to-be-done | T19 | 2 to 3 |
| How-to articles per pillar | T20 | 10 to 20 |
| Glossary (category vocabulary) | T22 | 15 to 30 terms |
| Original research / benchmark report | T24 | 1 |
| Free tool or calculator | T25 | 1 |
| Templates | T26 | 2 to 5 |
| Public docs / help center | T27 | as needed |
| Changelog | T28 | 1 |
| Reviews hub (G2/Capterra embeds + links) | T18 | 1 |

### Ongoing
- 4 to 8 pieces/month across: comparisons (whenever a competitor changes pricing/features), how-tos, research.
- One flagship data piece per quarter (survey, benchmark, usage data). This is the mention engine.
- Update comparison and pricing pages every quarter with a dated "last verified".

### Off-site priorities
1. **G2, Capterra, GetApp, Software Advice, TrustRadius, Product Hunt**: complete profiles, category placement, steady review flow (in-app prompts after success moments).
2. **Third-party "best X software" listicles**: find every list AI cites for your category (BrandPulse citation gap), contact authors with a briefing, data, and a free account. This is usually the single highest-leverage AI move for SaaS.
3. **Reddit**: founder/team accounts with real names, answering questions in relevant subreddits, disclosing affiliation. Never astroturf.
4. **YouTube**: product walkthroughs, comparison videos, tutorial series (YouTube is a top-cited domain in AI Overviews and Perplexity).
5. **LinkedIn**: founder thought leadership; LinkedIn is heavily cited by Copilot and appears in top cited domains overall.
6. **Integration partner directories** (Zapier, Slack, HubSpot marketplaces, etc.).
7. **Podcasts, newsletters, guest data pieces** in the category.
8. **Wikipedia/Wikidata**: only when genuinely notable with independent coverage; create/complete a Wikidata item and Crunchbase profile earlier.

### Day-90 page count: 70 to 120

## D. E-commerce / D2C brand

### Launch set (days 1 to 30)
| Page | Template | Count |
|---|---|---|
| Homepage | T1 | 1 |
| Collection pages for every real product type | T29 | 5 to 20 |
| Product pages (complete, unique descriptions; no manufacturer copy-paste) | T8 | all SKUs |
| About / brand story / sourcing | T2 | 1 |
| Shipping, returns, refunds, warranty policies | T6 | 3 to 4 |
| Reviews hub | T18 | 1 |
| Contact / support / FAQ | T5 | 1 to 2 |
| Size/fit/ingredient guides as relevant | T21 | 1 to 5 |

### Expansion (days 31 to 90)
| Page | Template | Count |
|---|---|---|
| Buying guides ("best [product] for [need]") | T30 | 5 to 10 |
| Need-based collections ("for oily skin", "for flat feet", "under 999") | T29 | 5 to 15 |
| Comparisons (your product vs category alternatives, ingredient A vs B) | T12 | 3 to 8 |
| Ingredient / material / technology glossary | T22 | 10 to 20 |
| How-to / routine / care guides | T20 | 5 to 10 |

### Agent and feed priorities (AAO matters most here)
1. **Google Merchant Center** feed complete and error-free: GTIN, brand, MPN, price, availability, shipping, returns, product highlights, multiple images. Feeds Google Shopping, AI Mode shopping and agentic checkout.
2. **OpenAI product feed** (ChatGPT merchants program) per OpenAI's feed spec, if eligible in the client's market.
3. **Platform agentic commerce** settings (Shopify's UCP-based and agent channels, etc.) switched on and checked.
4. Product pages: price, stock, variants, shipping time, return window **in HTML**, plus `Product`/`Offer` schema with `shippingDetails` and `hasMerchantReturnPolicy`.
5. Reviews with photos on product pages; syndicate to Google via product ratings.

### Off-site priorities
- YouTube reviews and creator seeding (send product to reviewers, never pay for positive verdicts without disclosure).
- Reddit category communities.
- Marketplaces (Amazon, Flipkart, Nykaa, Myntra) with consistent titles and strong reviews: AI often cites marketplace pages.
- Editorial "best X" roundups (outreach with samples).

### Day-90 page count: catalog + 30 to 60 content pages

## E. Professional services, non-local

*Agency, consultancy, B2B services, law/accounting firm selling nationally.*

| Phase | Pages |
|---|---|
| Launch | Home (T1), About (T2), Team pages (T4), Services hub (T7), Service pages (T8) x 5 to 10, Pricing or engagement models with ranges (T9), Case studies (T17) x 3 to 5, Industries (T15) x 3 to 5, Contact (T5), Brand facts (T3) |
| Expansion | Cost guides (T23) for each service, "how to choose a [service] agency" guide, comparisons (in-house vs agency, agency vs freelancer, approach A vs B) (T12), pillar guides (T19) x 2, original research (T24), question articles (T21) x 10 |
| Ongoing | 2 to 4 expert articles/month bylined by named experts; quarterly research; webinar/podcast transcripts |

**Off-site:** Clutch, GoodFirms, DesignRush, UpCity reviews; LinkedIn founder content; guest articles and podcasts; speaking; awards; partner directories (Google Partner, HubSpot Partner, etc.).

### Day-90 page count: 40 to 70

## F. Marketplace / directory / platform

The one archetype where large-scale (programmatic) page generation is legitimate, **if every page carries unique data**.

| Page type | Rule |
|---|---|
| Listing detail pages (each provider/property/job) | Unique data from the listing itself; noindex thin or expired listings |
| Category x location pages ("dentists in Saket") | Only index when there are enough listings (for example 3+) and unique aggregated data (price ranges, ratings, counts) |
| Category hubs, location hubs | Strong internal linking down to detail pages |
| Guides per category (cost, how to choose) | T23, T19 |
| Data/stats pages (market prices by city) | T24: the marketplace's unique data is its mention engine |

**Technical musts:** crawl budget control (faceted navigation, parameters), XML sitemaps split by type, handling of expired listings (410 or redirect to parent), canonicalization, internal linking at scale.

## G. Publisher / media / creator / info business

- Topic clusters: 3 to 6 core topics, each with a pillar (T19), 15 to 40 supporting articles, glossary (T22).
- Author pages with credentials for every writer (T4).
- Original reporting, data, first-hand testing: the only defensible moat as AI summarizes generic info.
- YouTube and newsletter as primary channels; the site as the canonical archive.
- Update or consolidate decaying articles every quarter (content pruning).

## H. Hospitality / travel / experiences

- Home, rooms/experiences pages (T8) with prices and availability, location page with "how to reach", things-to-do guides near the property (T19, T20), policies (T6), booking (T5), reviews hub (T18).
- **Off-site is dominant:** Google Business Profile and Google Hotels, TripAdvisor, Booking.com, Airbnb, MakeMyTrip/Agoda, travel blogs and YouTube.
- Schema: `Hotel`/`LodgingBusiness`, `TouristAttraction`, `Event`, `Offer`.
- Agents: real-time availability and a direct booking engine readable without login.

## I. Education / training

- Program pages (T8) with fees, duration, schedule, eligibility, syllabus, faculty (T4), outcomes data (placement %, scores, with methodology).
- Fees page (T9), batch/schedule page, centre pages (T10) if multi-location.
- Comparison pages (online vs offline, course A vs B), "is [course] worth it" (T21), exam guides (T19), cost guides (T23).
- Student success stories (T17) with names/consent.
- Off-site: Shiksha, CollegeDunia, Careers360 (India), Course Report/SwitchUp (bootcamps), Reddit, YouTube, Quora.
- Schema: `Course`, `EducationalOrganization`, `Event` for batches.

## J. Healthcare provider / YMYL

Same structure as A or B, with stricter rules:

- **Every** treatment/condition page reviewed by a named, qualified doctor (visible "Medically reviewed by Dr. X, MDS, [registration no.], on [date]").
- Doctor pages (T4) with registration/licence numbers, qualifications, experience, specializations.
- Treatment pages (T8) must include risks, contraindications, recovery, cost range, and cite medical sources.
- Condition/symptom guides (T19/T21) only with medical review; avoid diagnosis claims.
- No outcome guarantees, no before/after without consent, follow local advertising rules for medical practices (in India, check NMC/dental council norms).
- Off-site: Practo, Lybrate, hospital/clinic directories, Google reviews, medical association listings, doctor interviews in media.

## Universal add-ons (every archetype, once basics are done)

| Page | Why |
|---|---|
| Brand facts page (T3) | Single source of truth for AI and press |
| HTML sitemap page (`/sitemap`) | Every page one click from somewhere; helps crawlers and agents |
| Glossary (T22) | Topical authority + entity associations |
| "How to choose a [category]" guide | Wins the evaluation fan-out; position your strengths as criteria |
| Cost guide (T23) for the main offer | Cost is sub-queried in almost every commercial AI answer |
| One original data asset (T24) | Earns mentions, the strongest AI visibility correlate |
