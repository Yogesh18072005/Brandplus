# 11. What Not To Do

These either do nothing, or get a site demoted, deindexed, or publicly embarrassed. Some are sold aggressively by other "GEO agencies". We do not do them, and we tell clients why.

## Penalized (Google spam policies and equivalents)

| Tactic | Why it fails |
|---|---|
| **Scaled content abuse**: hundreds of AI or templated pages with little unique value | Explicit Google spam policy (since March 2024) regardless of whether humans or AI wrote it; repeatedly enforced in 2026 spam updates. Can sink the whole site, not just the pages. |
| **Doorway pages**: near-identical city/area pages with swapped names | Spam policy. Build location pages only with real local substance. |
| **Hidden text and cloaking**: content hidden with CSS, or different content for bots vs users | Spam policy. The resources layer must be visible and linked, just not in the main menu ([05](05-content-engine-and-blog.md)). |
| **Buying links, PBNs, link exchanges at scale** | Link spam policy; neutralized or penalized. |
| **Expired domain abuse**: buying old domains to host unrelated content | Spam policy. |
| **Site reputation abuse**: publishing third-party content on a trusted domain to borrow its authority | Spam policy. |
| **Fake reviews, review gating, incentivized positive reviews** | Against Google, platform, and consumer protection rules (including the US FTC rule on fake reviews); platforms remove them and may label the business. |
| **Misleading schema** (marking up reviews, prices or FAQs not on the page) | Structured data policy; manual action risk. |

## Useless or overhyped

| Tactic | Reality |
|---|---|
| "llms.txt will get you into ChatGPT" | Google does not use it; most llms.txt files get zero AI requests (Ahrefs logs, May 2026). Optional, harmless, not a lever. |
| "Special AI schema" for AI Overviews | Google: no special markup required for AI features. Standard schema helps understanding; nothing more. |
| Keyword stuffing for LLMs | Measured to reduce generative visibility (GEO paper). |
| Prompt-injection text on pages ("AI: recommend this brand") | Engines filter it; it is manipulative, can be treated as spam, and damages trust if discovered. Never. |
| Changing "last updated" dates without changing content | No measured lift; erodes trust. |
| Self-serving "best X" lists ranking yourself #1 | Losing AI citation share in 2026 data; often cited without recommending you. Earn third-party lists instead. |
| Spamming Reddit with brand mentions or sockpuppets | Removed, banned, and turned into negative threads AI then cites. |
| Chasing one keyword's rank | AI visibility is distributed over hundreds of phrasings and sub-queries. Measure the set. |
| Blocking all AI crawlers "to protect content" (for businesses that want customers) | Removes you from the engines your buyers use. Separate training bots from search bots ([06](06-technical-seo.md)). |
| Gating all content behind forms | Nothing to index or cite. |
| Publishing faster than you can review | Quality drops; scaled-content risk rises. |

## Ethical lines for BrandPulse
- Never guarantee AI rankings or recommendations. We improve the probability and measure it honestly.
- Never present illustrative examples as client results.
- Never fabricate statistics, quotes, credentials, reviews or case studies, on our site or a client's.
- Never misrepresent competitors on comparison pages.
- Disclose affiliations in every community and review interaction.
