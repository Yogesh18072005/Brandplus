# 02. Diagnose the Business First

The page plan, content plan and off-site plan all come from the diagnosis. Two businesses in the same industry can need completely different sites: a single-clinic dentist and a 400-clinic dental chain share a category but not a strategy.

Spend 60 to 90 minutes here per client. The output is a one-page **Discovery Brief**.

---

## Step 1. Answer the 20 diagnosis questions

### Identity
1. What is the business called, exactly, and what other names does it go by (abbreviations, old names, founder's name)?
2. In one sentence: what do they sell, to whom, where?
3. What category would a buyer type into a search box? (Use the buyer's words, not the founder's. "Cosmetic dentist", not "smile design atelier".)
4. What are the 3 to 5 competitors that buyers actually compare them against? (Ask the founder, then verify with a BrandPulse audit: AI may name different competitors.)

### Offer
5. List every product, service, plan, or SKU group. Which 20 percent drive 80 percent of revenue?
6. What does it cost? Is pricing public? (If not, can a range be published?)
7. What is the buying cycle: impulse (minutes), considered (days/weeks), committee (months)?
8. Who is the buyer, and who is the user (if different)? List 2 to 4 personas.

### Geography
9. Where do customers come from: one neighborhood, one city, many cities, one country, global?
10. Physical locations: none, one, or many? Service area business (goes to the customer) or storefront (customer comes)?

### Proof
11. Reviews: count and rating on Google, and on the category's review platforms.
12. Case studies, credentials, awards, certifications, press, notable clients.
13. Original data the business has that nobody else does (pricing data, outcomes, usage stats, survey data).

### Current visibility
14. Is the site indexed? How many pages? (`site:domain.com` plus Search Console.)
15. What does it rank for today? (Search Console queries, or a rank tool.)
16. BrandPulse audit: presence rate, share of voice, missed prompts, citation gap.
17. Which off-site sources does AI cite in this category (BrandPulse citation gap), and is the brand on them?

### Constraints
18. Regulated? (Health, finance, legal, insurance = YMYL: stricter E-E-A-T, medical/legal review needed.)
19. Who can produce content: founder, team, agency, us? How many hours per week?
20. Tech stack: WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, Next.js, Wix? What can be changed?

## Step 2. Classify the archetype

Pick the **one primary archetype** that matches how buyers find and choose the business. Most businesses also have a secondary.

| # | Archetype | Examples | How buyers search | Discovery center of gravity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | **Local service, single location** | Dentist, salon, CA firm, gym, restaurant, plumber | "best X in [area]", "X near me", "X cost [city]" | Google Business Profile, reviews, local directories, service pages |
| B | **Multi-location local** | Dental chain, coaching institute chain, clinic network, franchise | Same as A, per branch | Location pages + GBP per branch + brand authority |
| C | **B2B SaaS / software** | CRM, dev tool, AI tool, HR software | "best X software for Y", "X vs Y", "X alternatives", "how to do [job]" | Comparison/alternatives pages, G2/Capterra, Reddit, docs, integrations |
| D | **E-commerce / D2C brand** | Skincare, apparel, electronics, food brand | "best [product] for [need]", "[product] under [price]", brand + review | Product/collection pages, merchant feeds, reviews, YouTube, Reddit, marketplaces |
| E | **Professional services, non-local** | Agency, consultancy, law firm serving a country, B2B services | "best [service] agency for [industry]", "how much does X cost" | Expertise content, case studies, thought leadership, directories (Clutch), LinkedIn |
| F | **Marketplace / directory / platform** | Real estate portal, job board, booking platform, comparison site | "[thing] in [place]", "[category] [filter]" | Programmatic (but substantive) listing pages, internal linking, data |
| G | **Publisher / media / creator / info business** | Blog, newsletter, course creator, community | Informational queries, "how to", "what is" | Topical depth, authority, original reporting, YouTube |
| H | **Hospitality / travel / experiences** | Hotel, tour operator, venue, restaurant group | "best [stay/activity] in [place]", "[place] itinerary" | OTAs, TripAdvisor, Google Hotels/Maps, guides, photos |
| I | **Education / training** | Coaching institute, bootcamp, university program, course | "best [course] in [city/online]", "[exam] coaching fees", "is X worth it" | Program pages, outcomes data, reviews, Reddit, YouTube |
| J | **Healthcare provider / YMYL specialist** | Hospital, specialist doctor, clinic, therapist | Symptom, treatment, cost, doctor name, "best [specialist] in [city]" | Doctor pages with credentials, treatment pages reviewed by doctors, Practo/health directories, GBP |

Notes:
- J is A or B with stricter YMYL rules. Treat it as its own archetype because content requirements change (medical review, credentials, citations to medical sources).
- A SaaS founder who sells mainly via services is C primary, E secondary.

## Step 3. Apply the modifiers

Modifiers change the volume and type of pages within the archetype.

| Modifier | Values | Effect on the plan |
|---|---|---|
| **Geography** | Hyperlocal / city / multi-city / national / global / multilingual | Decides location pages, hreflang, local directories, currency/language |
| **Brand maturity** | New (under 1 year, little demand) / growing / established | New brands need off-site mentions and long-tail pages first; established brands defend branded queries and comparison pages |
| **Competition** | Low / medium / high (look at who ranks and who AI names) | High competition = more depth per page, more off-site, longer timeline |
| **YMYL** | Yes / no | Yes = expert authors, reviewer bylines, sources, stricter claims |
| **Buying cycle** | Impulse / considered / committee | Considered and committee = comparison, pricing, case studies, ROI, security pages |
| **Catalog size** | 1 to 10 offers / 10 to 100 / 100+ | Large catalogs need category architecture, faceted navigation control, feeds |
| **Content capacity** | Low (under 2 h/week) / medium / high | Decides publishing cadence; never plan more than the team can do *well* |
| **Price transparency** | Public / range / quote-only | Quote-only businesses still need cost-guide pages with ranges; agents skip "call for price" |

## Step 4. Build the query universe

This is the list of buyer questions the site must win. It becomes both the page plan and the BrandPulse audit prompt set.

### The 9 intent buckets (cover all of them)

| Bucket | Pattern | Example (cosmetic dentist, Delhi) | Example (B2B SaaS, CRM) | Page type that wins |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Category best | best/top X (in place / for Y) | best cosmetic dentist in Delhi | best CRM for small agencies | Service/category page + off-site lists |
| 2. Need-specific | X for [problem/need] | where to get veneers in Delhi | CRM with WhatsApp integration | Service detail / feature / use case page |
| 3. Cost / price | how much / cost / price / fees | veneers cost in Delhi | HubSpot pricing vs Pipedrive | Pricing page + cost guide |
| 4. Comparison | X vs Y, X or Y | veneers vs bonding | Pipedrive vs HubSpot | Comparison page |
| 5. Alternatives | alternatives to X, apps like X | Clove Dental alternatives | HubSpot alternatives | Alternatives page |
| 6. Evaluation / trust | is X good, X reviews, X legit | is Nova Dental good | Zoho CRM reviews | Reviews hub, case studies, off-site reviews |
| 7. How-to / education | how to, what is, guide | how long do veneers last | how to set up a sales pipeline | Guides, FAQ, glossary |
| 8. Local / near me | near me, in [area], open now | dentist near Saket open Sunday | (n/a) | Location page + GBP |
| 9. Action | book, buy, sign up, demo, contact | book teeth whitening appointment Delhi | CRM free trial | Booking/pricing/signup page, agent-ready |

### How to generate it

1. **Seed** from the founder: what do customers ask on the first call?
2. **Expand** with: Google autocomplete, People Also Ask, related searches, Search Console queries, AnswerThePublic-style tools, Reddit and Quora threads, competitor page titles, sales call notes, support tickets.
3. **Fan-out**: for each core query, list the 5 to 10 follow-up questions an AI would sub-query (cost, time, risk, alternatives, who it is for, how to choose, what to avoid, local options).
4. **Cluster** by intent and topic. Each cluster maps to one page (or one section on a page).
5. **Prioritize** by: buying intent (bucket 1, 3, 4, 5, 9 first), revenue of the offer, current gap (BrandPulse missed prompts), and difficulty.

### Output target
- Local single-location: 60 to 150 queries, 15 to 30 clusters
- SaaS: 150 to 500 queries, 40 to 120 clusters
- E-commerce: per category, 20 to 50 queries; plus brand-level
- Use 40 of the highest-intent ones as the BrandPulse audit set and recheck monthly.

## Step 5. Write the Discovery Brief (one page)

Illustrative example (Nova Dental is our fictional anchor business; numbers are made up to show the format):

```
BUSINESS:        Nova Dental Studio (also: Nova Dental, Dr. A. Mehta's clinic)
ONE-LINER:       Cosmetic and general dentistry clinic in South Delhi for adults 25-55.
ARCHETYPE:       A (local single location), modifier J (YMYL: health)
GEOGRAPHY:       South Delhi primary (Saket, Malviya Nagar, Hauz Khas), Delhi NCR secondary
MATURITY:        Growing (6 years, 180 Google reviews, 4.8)
COMPETITION:     High (Clove Dental, Sabka Dentist, Dr. Batra's dominate AI answers)
TOP OFFERS:      Veneers, aligners, implants, whitening, root canal
PRICE:           Ranges publishable
COMPETITORS AI NAMES:  Clove Dental (29/40), Sabka Dentist (18/40), ...
CITATION GAP:    practo.com, justdial.com, lybrate.com, reddit.com/r/delhi, youtube.com
CURRENT STATE:   12 pages indexed, no service pages, no prices, no doctor page
CONTENT CAPACITY: 3 h/week from clinic + us
PLAN:            See blueprint A + J. 42 pages in 90 days. Off-site: Practo, Justdial,
                 GBP overhaul, review engine, 3 local media mentions, Reddit presence.
```
