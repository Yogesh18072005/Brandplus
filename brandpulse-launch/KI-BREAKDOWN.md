## The grammar

1. **Something physical is always moving through the frame**: a container, a dot on a rail, a route head, an arc, a cursor. The camera follows it.
2. **Every cut is motivated by an object.** A badge grows into a disc, a progress bar becomes a line, a pill expands, the frame shrinks into a card, bars shatter.
3. **Real product UI**, cropped big and tilted in 3D, with status pills, stamps and toasts.
4. **One coloured word per line**, plus an inline icon chip.
5. **Information worlds**: rail, route floor, terminal, map, globe and stat mosaics. Each step of the story gets a different world.
6. **Photography/3D renders appear at the hook and at the proof.** Everything else is designed UI.
