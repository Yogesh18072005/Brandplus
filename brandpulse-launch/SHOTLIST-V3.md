# BrandPulse launch film — v3 shot list

60 s · 1920×1080 · 30 fps · Remotion (`BrandPulse-Launch-V3`). Brand: Scan Blue `#1E5AE8`, ink `#0A1222`, paper `#F6F7F4`, mint `#A9FFDF`, green dot `#1FC58A`. Fonts: Manrope (300 vs 800 in the same line) + IBM Plex Mono. Source of facts: `public/llms.txt`, PRODUCT_STRATEGY.md. Brands, answers and scores in the demo UI are fictional and labelled "Illustrative example".

**Plant → payoff:** the "Your brand · NOT MENTIONED" row (S04) is scanned into "Gap found · fixable" (S10). The ten red tiles (S07) flip to "Named" after the re-check (S18).

Every shot has a far layer (blurred gradient/bokeh), a sharp subject in a CSS-3D world, and a blurred near object crossing the lens. The camera always moves and adds handheld drift. Cuts land on the 15-frame grid or on a VO word.

## Sound

No music. Voice: `en-US-AndrewMultilingualNeural` (edge-tts), with word timings in `src/v3/words.json` driving every text hit. SFX are generated by `scripts/make-sound-v3.py`:

- One cut sound per transition, shaped by type, with the whoosh crest on the cut.
- 10 signature sub-drop + impact hits.
- One riser into "Meet".
- 11 quiet UI cues where the eye is.
- A shared short room tail.
- About 12 dB under the VO, with 6 dB ducking while the voice is speaking.

## Rebuild

```
python -I scripts/make-narration-v3.py    # VO + word timings
python -I scripts/make-sound-v3.py        # SFX + final mix
npx remotion render BrandPulse-Launch-V3 outputs/brandpulse-launch-v3.mp4 --codec=h264 --crf=16
```
